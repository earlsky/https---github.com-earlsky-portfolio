That’s a great project! You’re essentially designing a **fire control system (FCS)** for an AR-15, which requires integrating **hardware (sensors, actuators, microcontroller/FPGA) and software (embedded programming, ballistics calculations, UI for adjustments).** Here’s how you should approach it:

---

### **1. Define System Requirements**
Your system should:
- **Measure Distance to Target:** Using **LiDAR, radar, or a laser rangefinder.**
- **Measure Environmental Factors:** Wind speed, temperature, humidity, barometric pressure.
- **Calculate Ballistics:** Account for bullet drop, spin drift, Coriolis effect, and wind deflection.
- **Make Adjustments:** Either **automated (servo-motor system)** or **manual (display guidance to shooter).**
- **User Interface:** OLED display, HUD, or mobile app for data visualization.

---

### **2. Hardware Selection**
- **Microcontroller or FPGA**  
  - Arduino (simple), STM32 (real-time, robust), or Raspberry Pi (for ML integration).  
  - FPGA (if you need ultra-fast real-time computations).  
- **Sensors**  
  - **Laser Rangefinder (LIDAR)** – Measures target distance.  
  - **Inertial Measurement Unit (IMU)** – Detects rifle tilt, angle, and movement.  
  - **Wind Sensor (Anemometer)** – Measures wind speed and direction.  
  - **Barometric Sensor** – Helps with altitude calculations.  
- **Servo Motors (Optional for Auto-Adjustments)**  
  - High-precision stepper motors to adjust rifle angle automatically.  

---

### **3. Software & Algorithm Development**
- **Embedded Programming:** C/C++ (for microcontrollers), Python (for rapid prototyping).  
- **Ballistics Calculation:** Implement or integrate a **ballistic solver (e.g., JBM Ballistics, Applied Ballistics API).**  
- **Sensor Fusion:** Combine sensor inputs for real-time data.  
- **Machine Learning (Optional):** Predict projectile path based on past shots.  

---

### **4. System Workflow**
1. **Target Distance Measurement** (Laser rangefinder).  
2. **Environmental Factor Collection** (Wind, temperature, pressure).  
3. **Angle & Tilt Measurement** (IMU).  
4. **Ballistic Calculation** (Trajectory adjustments based on physics).  
5. **Adjustment Mechanism**  
   - **Manual:** Display elevation/windage adjustments to shooter.  
   - **Automated:** Servo-controlled aiming system.  

---

### **5. Prototyping & Testing**
- **Phase 1:** Build a basic **sensor system** and test distance measurement.  
- **Phase 2:** Integrate **ballistic calculations** and validate accuracy.  
- **Phase 3:** Test in **real-world shooting conditions** and refine software/hardware.
