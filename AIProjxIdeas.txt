1 Building MULTI-AGENT system w CrewAI
---
A multi-agent system is a group of AI agents that collaborate, divide tasks, and solve complex problems together, just like a team.

Tech stacks:
CrewAI for multi-agent coordination
LangChain for tool-building
OpenAI / Claude / Llama3 for LLMs
Pinecone / Chroma for memory


2 Designing MULTIMODAL AI Model
---
Multimodal AI models can simultaneously understand and generate content from multiple data types, including images, text, audio, and video.

Tech stacks:
Hugging Face Transformers for CLIP
PyTorch or TensorFlow
Libra for audio feature extraction
OpenAI Whisper for speech-to-text


3 Developing a PREDICTIVE KEYBOARD Model
---
A predictive keyboard learns from past text to suggest the next word or phrase.

Tech stacks:
PyTorch or TensorFlow
Tokenizers from Hugging Face
Datasets: WikiText, or Sherlock Holmes novels


4 Analyzing LARGE DOCUMENTS with LLMs
---
It involves processing documents of 100+ pages, such as legal contracts, to extracts summaries, FAQs, and key insights.

Tech stacks:
LangChain + OpenAI / Cohere
ChromaDB / Pinecone for vector search
Transformers (BART, T5, Longformer)
PyMuPDF / PDFPlumber for PDF parsing
